One grand entrance,three more ways in.

The Grand Entrance is the main design and opens the site. Options A, B and C are other directions for the same homepage. All four share the same stores, the same specials and the same portal underneath, so you are choosing the look and the welcome, not the features.

One portal behind every design

Whichever homepage you pick, staff, applicants and managers use the same portal. Open any part below and choose a demo role; no password is needed.

  • Hiring portalOpen jobs by store, a ten-minute application, ID and document uploads, and a status page for every applicant.
  • Staff portalShifts for the week, leave requests and balances, payslips, training and documents.
  • HR consoleThe hiring board, document checks, interviews and offers, and expiring food badges.
  • Store adminSpecials, social posts, store hours and staff notices, published from one place.
