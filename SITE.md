## Layout (next-rebuild)
- `/` the main design, Grand Entrance (`app/(home)/`, `styles/home.css`, `lib/home/`, scene generator in `tools/grand-entrance/`)
- The inner pages and the portal: `/specials`, `/departments`, `/wholesale`, `/locations`, `/about`, `/careers`, `/portal`, `/hr`, `/admin`.
- `/tour` the guided tour (2026-10-29): eight chapters, each played as the person who would do it (shopper, office, HR, staff member). It signs in as that person, points at the real control and says what to try. Engine `lib/tour.ts` + `components/tour/`, chapters `lib/tour-chapters.ts`; linked from the concept banner and the home footer. Its "reset the demo first" box is on by default, so each chapter finds the data it expects.
- Staff updates, the internal newsletter (2026-10-29): the office writes one at `/admin#staffnews` (HR also at `/hr#updates`), picks stores and teams, and it goes to each person's work address and their portal inbox at `/portal/updates`. An update can ask staff to confirm they read it; the sender sees who has not and can remind only those people. It has no unsubscribe link and never touches the shoppers' list. A sender whose access covers some stores can only pick those stores (Marsha, Princes Town only, shows this). Code `lib/staff-updates.ts`, seed `lib/seed-staff-updates.ts` (staff tier, never in the public bundle).
- Members' offers and codes (2026-10-29): `/offers` and `/offers/[slug]`, passwordless shopper sign-in at `/account/sign-in` (a one-use 15-minute link), the wallet at `/account`, and the till check at `/portal/till`. Codes are random, stored only as a hash plus their last four characters, scoped to stores and dates, and checked at the till with reasons for every refusal (five refusals in ten minutes lock that till for five minutes). Offers are drafted by marketing and approved by someone else (`/admin#promos`); redemptions by store at `/admin#codes`. Code `lib/promo.ts`, `lib/members.ts`, `lib/account.ts`, `components/members/`.
- Weekly e-mail (2026-10-29): sign-up on `/specials` and in the inner pages' footer, double opt-in, a consent ledger, preferences and one-click unsubscribe at `/newsletter/*`. Campaigns are built from the specials at `/admin#newsletter` and go only to confirmed subscribers for the chosen stores and topics. Marketing sees the list with addresses masked; the owner's office sees full addresses and can export a CSV, which is logged. Code `lib/newsletter.ts`.
- Demo outbox `/outbox`: every e-mail the demo would send (confirmations, sign-in links, newsletters, staff updates, hiring updates). Nothing leaves the browser.
- Access and audit (2026-10-29): grants by person, role, stores and end date (nine in the seed, Ria's old HR grant revoked) (`lib/access.ts`, `lib/seed-staff.ts`); revoke and restore with a reason at `/admin#access`; every approval, export, till check, sign-in and access change in `/admin#audit`. Each write re-reads the session and grant when it runs. The office console opens on `/admin#today`, a list worked out from that person's access.
- Hiring decisions (2026-10-29): HR's Decision step previews the applicant's letter and keeps HR's reason apart from it; the applicant gets an "update" e-mail and a sealed card that looks the same for an offer or a "not selected" (`components/careers/outcome-card.tsx`, `components/hr/decision-form.tsx`, `lib/hr/outcome.ts`).
- Data tiers: the staff, member and mailing-list seeds (`lib/seed-staff.ts`, `lib/seed-members.ts`, `lib/seed-mailing.ts`, `lib/seed-staff-updates.ts`) load only on signed-in pages through `lib/private.ts`; the shopper pages load the member tier on demand (`lib/account.ts`). After a build, a scratch check of the client chunks found none of their strings on any public route (2026-10-29: 0 of 16 public routes). In production these become server-side reads behind row-level security.
- `docs/plan/value-ladder.md` the value and pricing brief: what this build is worth as a product, each figure labelled computed, estimated or researched.
- Old `/options/…` links redirect to `/`. The department layout switch is gone too; the home shows the tiles.
- Trinovata credit in the home footer's legal row: the on-dark triangle mark (`public/assets/img/trinovata-mark-ondark.png`, 64 px copy of `Products/Trinovata-Brand/brand/01-logo/trinovata-mark-ondark-alpha.png`) and "Created by Trinovata Technologies", linking to trinovata.com. Styled to the brand book: #F2F0EB words in its Label style (Archivo 600 13 px, 0.12em, uppercase), 16 px mark with 8 px clear space, no scrim or box on the solid footer. The book covers the on-dark mark on ink, not on the footer's green (#123F22); Gregg chose to keep it on 2026-10-29, over the white mark. The brand book only approves "Engineered with"; Gregg asked for "Created by" in chat on 2026-10-24.
