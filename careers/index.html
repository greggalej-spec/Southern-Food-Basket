How hiring works

  1. Apply

    Five short steps online. Save and come back any time.

  2. Screening

    HR reads your application within three working days.

  3. Documents (KYC)

    We check your ID, BIR, NIS and references. You see what is still missing.

  4. Interview

    At the branch you applied to, with the team lead.

  5. Offer

    Pay, start date and hours in writing. Accept or decline in your dashboard.
