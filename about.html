The South’s basket

A supermarket and wholesale business in south Trinidad, and a proposal for what its website, hiring and staff tools could look like.

What the public record says

Southern Food Basket is a grocer in south Trinidad. Public directories place branches in San Fernando, Princes Town and Penal, and trade records say a little about what it buys and sells. Each line below carries its source. Everything else on this site is a proposal, not a description.

    About this concept

    This site is an unsolicited prototype built by Trinovata Technologies (opens in a new tab) on 22 October 2026, to show what a grocer’s public website, hiring portal and employee portal could look like as one system.

    It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or commissioned by Southern Food Basket Ltd. Every person, job, price, phone number, e-mail address and document on it is demo data. Branch addresses are from public directories and have not been verified. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
